## Experiment Objective

In this experiment, I will attempt to perform basic interpositioning to keep track of allocation generations, life status (i.e. allocated, free, reused), size, base address, etc. Due to the limitations of interpositionining, this experiment will work with the assumption that only 4 allocations at any given time are active. Because of the way tcache works, we will also assume that the 4 allocations are the ones that will be reused over and over, so we won't need to track more than 4 allocations at any given time.



Because there is a startup function that uses heap space, I will add a global flag in `main.c` that upon being reached and set, will allow our malloc() wrapper to begin tracking allocations.
